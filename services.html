Job Work Workflow

The Precision Job Work Process

A structured technical roadmap from customer drawing review to precision-inspected dispatch.

01

Drawing & Specification Review

Thorough evaluation of customer 2D blueprints or 3D CAD models, critical tolerances, taper angles, and material requirements.

02

CAM Programming & Start Hole Prep

Generating optimized Wire EDM cutting paths and executing Sparkonix super drilling (0.3–3.0 mm) for wire entry holes where required.

03

Precision Setup & First Cut Inspection

Rigid workholding fixture alignment on FANUC Wire EDM bed with electronic edge-finding and initial trial verification.

04

Wire EDM & Sparking Execution

High-accuracy wire cutting and spark erosion with controlled flushing, delivering fine surface finishes and tight dimensional control.

05

Dimensional Inspection & Job Dispatch

Rigorous verification using TRIMOS V5, micrometers, and precision gauges to ensure 100% drawing compliance prior to job delivery.
